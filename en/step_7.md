## Attach the micro:bit 

--- task ---
### Use card for the board

Attach the micro:bit board to a bit of cardboard with an elastic or hairband.

![micro:bit attached to a piece of card with a hairband.](images/attach-2.jpg){:width="500px"}
--- /task ---

--- task ---
### Organise the wires

Tuck some of the wires under the band on the other side of the card to keep them tidy.

![Wires on a bit of card tucked into a hairband.](images/attach-3.jpg){:width="500px"}
--- /task ---


--- task ---
### Attach to sock

Glue, sew, or tape the card to the sock.

![Side view of the micro:bit and wires attached to the sock puppet.](images/attach-7.png){:width="500px"}
![Front view of the micro:bit and wires attached to the sock puppet.](images/attach-8.png){:width="500px"}
--- /task ---

**Test:** Your puppet is now ready to go! Try it out to see if the sounds play when the mouth opens. You can also change the sound blocks to create different characters.
