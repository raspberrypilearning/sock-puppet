## Add hair

--- task ---
### Make the hair

Wrap coloured yarn or string around some scrap card lots of times. 

![Yarn wrapped around card many times.](images/hair-1.png){:width="500px"}
--- /task ---


--- task ---
Take the yarn off the card and tie it up with another bit of yarn.  

Tie a knot in the middle of the yarn to create two loops.

![Yarn taken off the card, but still in a loop.](images/hair-3.png){:width="500px"}
![Tying a knot in the middle of the loop to create two loops.](images/hair-4.png){:width="500px"}
--- /task ---


--- task ---
Cut the end of each loop.

![Cutting the yarn loops.](images/hair-5.png){:width="500px"}
--- /task ---


--- task ---
### Attach and style hair

Sew, tape, or glue the yarn onto the middle of the puppet's head. 

**Warning:** If using hot glue, do not touch the glue directly.

![Gluing the head of the puppet to attach the hair.](images/hair-6.png){:width="500px"}
![Pressing down the hair to fix onto glue.](images/hair-7.png){:width="500px"}
![Puppet with pink hair made of yarn.](images/hair-8.png){:width="500px"}
--- /task ---

Style the hair how you want it.
